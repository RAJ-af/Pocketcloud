# PocketCloud Terms & Conditions

**Draft version: 2026-10-24.** Legal review is required before public release.

## Service

PocketCloud is intended to help a user connect devices they own or are authorised to administer, using account authentication, device pairing and a personal file vault. Availability depends on device power, internet access, Android power management, Supabase, network traversal and optional relay services.

## Acceptable use

You agree to use only devices and files you own or have permission to access. You must not use PocketCloud for covert surveillance, unauthorised data collection, bypassing access controls, malware distribution, unlawful access, or exposing someone else's files. Do not attempt to break or overload the service, abuse pairing codes, or bypass per-device permissions.

## Your responsibilities

Keep account credentials private, protect the server phone, review pairings, disable server mode when not needed, revoke lost devices and maintain independent backups for important files. You are responsible for complying with privacy, copyright, data protection and computer access laws applicable to your use.

## Data and third-party services

Supabase handles authentication and cloud metadata. File payloads are intended to travel peer-to-peer using WebRTC; a TURN service may relay encrypted traffic. Third-party services may have availability limits or their own terms.

## No promise of uninterrupted service

The service is provided as an MVP and may fail on particular Android devices or networks. Auto-reconnect cannot restore a powered-off or force-stopped phone. Do not rely on PocketCloud as the only copy of important files or as an emergency, safety-critical or enterprise backup system.

## Suspension and termination

Access may be suspended for abuse or security risk. You may sign out, disable server mode, revoke devices and stop using the app. A self-service account deletion flow is not part of the initial MVP.

## Limitation and updates

This draft cannot determine mandatory legal rights or liability terms for every jurisdiction. Have qualified counsel finalise consumer rights, liability, governing law, dispute handling, provider details, age limits and contact information. Material changes should increment the policy version and be shown to the user for a new decision.
